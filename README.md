# Waythrough Public Feedback & Issue Tracker

Welcome, and thank you for helping test **Waythrough**.

This repository exists as a place for beta testers and early users to report bugs, suggest improvements, and share feedback about the app.

## What is Waythrough?

Waythrough is a task and planning app designed for people who often feel overwhelmed by traditional productivity systems.

Most productivity apps focus on organizing tasks.

Waythrough focuses on helping you figure out **what to do next**.

The goal is to help you:

* Capture thoughts, tasks, ideas, and commitments quickly
* Break large or intimidating projects into manageable next steps
* Return to unfinished work without losing context
* Stay focused without relying on willpower alone
* Move forward one small step at a time

While the app was inspired by challenges commonly experienced by people with ADHD and the shallow focus epidemic of our time, it is being built for anyone who has ever felt overwhelmed by complexity, procrastination, or too many competing priorities.

## What kind of feedback is helpful?

Bug reports are welcome, but they are only part of the picture.

We're especially interested in learning:

* What confused you?
* What felt intuitive?
* What felt frustrating?
* What did you expect the app to do?
* What were you trying to accomplish?
* What features feel missing?
* Where did you get stuck?

Feedback about the overall experience is often more valuable than technical details.

---

## Before submitting an issue

Please check whether someone has already reported the same problem.

If you're reporting a bug, include:

* What you were trying to do
* What happened
* What you expected to happen
* Screenshots if relevant
* Device and browser (if applicable)
